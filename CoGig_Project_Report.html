1. Executive Summary & Problem Landscape

1.1 The Gig Economy Crisis

Over 12 million informal service workers in India (plumbers, electricians, carpenters, painters, and domestic help) face systemic economic disenfranchisement on private venture-backed gig platforms (e.g., Urban Company, TaskRabbit). These platforms operate as extractive monopolies characterized by:

  • Extractive Take-Rates (20% to 30%): Workers lose nearly a third of their gross earnings in platform commissions and lead generation fees.
  • Opaque Algorithmic Deplatforming: Workers are permanently terminated without human recourse or due process based on unverified customer star ratings.
  • Zero Social Security: Classified as "independent contractors," gig workers lack health insurance, accidental death cover, or emergency liquidity.
  • Artificial Surge Price Gouging: Platforms exploit consumer distress during extreme weather and emergencies by hiking prices 1.5× to 3.0×, while capturing the surge windfall rather than passing it to workers.

1.2 The CoGig Cooperative Solution

CoGig replaces extractive corporate middlemen with a worker-owned platform cooperative governed by the International Cooperative Alliance (ICA) Principles and legally registered under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies (MSCS) Act. CoGig guarantees:

93% Direct Pay

The vast majority of every rupee paid by customers is settled directly and instantaneously into the worker's verified UPI account upon milestone sign-off.

5% Welfare Fund

Held in a member-governed trust pool providing ₹1,00,000 health insurance, ₹2,00,000 accidental life coverage (PMSBY), and emergency micro-loans.

2% Cost Recovery

Covers bare cloud server costs. All audited year-end surpluses are refunded to active workers as a Patronage Dividend (सहकारी लाभांश).

4. End-to-End Workflow & User Journeys

Workflow 1: Customer Booking to Instant Settlement & Review

1 Service & Schedule Selection: Customer selects trade (Plumbing), quantity (1 worker), date, and preferred slot (Morning 8-12 AM).
2 Location & Fare Estimation: Customer enters address; Leaflet verifies serviceability in Davangere Hub. AI calculates fair wage (₹550/day) with 0% surge markup.
3 Escrow Lock & Booking Creation: Customer clicks "Hire Worker & Send Booking Request". Funds are placed into digital escrow; booking appears on customer and admin ledgers.
4 Fair Allocation Dispatch: FWA algorithm assigns the most equitable, certified worker (Manjunath Gowda, NSQF-4 certified). Worker accepts via Worker Portal.
5 Service Execution & Proof of Work: Worker completes repair, uploads verification photos.
6 Escrow Release & Instant UPI Settlement: Customer taps "Approve Day 1 & Release Escrow". 93% (₹511.50) is instantly transferred to worker's UPI, 5% (₹27.50) to Welfare Fund, and 2% (₹11.00) to Platform.
7 Worker Review Modal: Modal opens automatically; customer rates 5 stars, selects compliment tags, and submits verified feedback.

Workflow 2: Ethical AI Fair Work Allocation (FWA) Engine

Fairness_Score = (0.30 × Wait_Time) + (0.25 × Income_Parity_Delta) + (0.20 × Skill_Match) + (0.15 × Distance_Inv) + (0.10 × Job_Count_Inv)
Component Weight Algorithmic Purpose & Social Equity Impact
Wait Time Score 30% Prioritizes members who have been waiting the longest. Watchdog triggers an emergency +0.40 priority boost if idle > 30 minutes.
Income Parity Delta 25% Calculates the gap between worker's monthly earnings and the cooperative target living wage, preventing algorithmic superstar concentration.
Skill & NSQF Match 20% Validates Skill India NSQF-4 certification for specialized trades (e.g. electrical hazards) to ensure citizen safety.
Proximity (Distance Inv) 15% Minimizes worker commute time and fuel expenses to sustain sub-30 minute SLAs.
Job Count Inverse 10% Balances the total number of monthly dispatches across the entire cooperative roster.
